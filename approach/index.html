Approach

Strategy is only useful if it can be run.

I don’t wait to be handed a plan. I diagnose the leak, set the roadmap, design the operating system, and stay close enough to execution that the strategy survives contact with the work.

01

Diagnose

Start with the funnel, the catalog, the account, or the product — not the channel. Find the actual constraint before anyone spends more.

02

Set the strategy

Positioning, roadmap, and success metrics that leadership, creative, and engineering can all use. Clear enough to sequence; specific enough to ship.

03

Design the system

SOPs, metadata, booking flows, AI agents, reporting cadences. The unglamorous layer that makes a strategy repeatable.

04

Ship and learn

I write the prompts, run the audits, and build in the codebase when that’s what the work needs. Then I turn results into the next test.

Practice areas

Where the work usually lives.

Account management

I came up owning the client relationship: aligning goals, translating audience data into recommendations, and keeping stakeholders in the same story. That discipline still sits under every later brief — if the account isn’t clear, the media and the product won’t be either.

Ad strategy

Paid social as a funnel, not a pile of ads. Meta and TikTok prospecting and retargeting, budget pacing on ROI signals, and creative testing that turns winners into playbooks. At Salt Creative that meant sub-$2 B2B leads and a $300+ average lead value.

AI enablement

I design agent systems with human gates: BettyOS for creative ops, structured SEO pipelines, catalog cleanup, and internal automations that cut manual work 40–70%. Prompt and context engineering, brand-voice guardrails, and enough code fluency to ship and test the workflow myself.

UX ideation & management

Navigation and merchandising rebuilt around shopper intent. PDP frameworks aimed at conversion and AOV. Booking UX, city landing pages, and onboarding flows. I treat UX as a growth surface: where the strategy meets the person trying to buy, book, or believe.

Growth systems

Full-funnel e-commerce, B2B SaaS GTM, marketing operations, analytics (GA4, ROAS, CAC, LTV, AOV), and brand positioning. The through-line is ownership: set the strategy, then build the machine that keeps it running.
